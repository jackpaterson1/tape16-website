FAQ

Common questions.

Common setup, download, license, and workflow questions for TAPE 16.

Is TAPE 16 a plugin?

No. TAPE 16 is a standalone Tape Machine DAW, not a plugin. It gives you a focused 16-track reel to reel style workflow inside its own app.

Where do I activate my serial?

Activate your serial inside the TAPE 16 app. The website account section is only for viewing active machines and deactivating a license if you no longer have access to that computer.

Can I try it before buying?

Yes. Download TAPE 16 and use the full-featured 7-day trial before buying a license.

What happens after I buy?

Your serial is delivered by email after checkout. Open TAPE 16, enter the serial inside the app, and activate it from there.

My serial email did not arrive. What should I do?

Check your spam, junk, promotions, and any other filtered folders in your email provider. Serial emails can sometimes be sorted away from your main inbox.

What platforms is TAPE 16 available on?

TAPE 16 is available for macOS, Windows and Linux. iOS and iPadOS are available in early access through TestFlight. See the download page for each platform.

What is the remote control feature?

The phone remote web app lets you control track arm, markers, rec safe, and transport from another device in a browser.

Is there really no undo?

Yes. TAPE 16 uses destructive, commit-first recording: recording over a section replaces the audio, and there is no undo. Because it does not keep hidden takes behind each edit, session files stay relatively small and are easy to share. Sessions using the stock plug-ins can be opened without collaborators needing matching third-party tools.

Can I use plugins in TAPE 16?

Yes. TAPE 16 supports modern plugin use inside a focused DAW workflow, so you can treat plugins like outboard gear while keeping the tape-machine feel.

Do you track what I record in TAPE 16?

No. Your recordings, sessions, audio files, and project data stay on your own machine. TAPE 16 does not upload your music, stems, takes, or session files to our servers.

Can I move my license to another computer?

Yes. If you no longer have access to an old machine, you can use the website account section to deactivate that machine and free up the license for another computer. You get 3 machines per license.

Are updates included?

Yes. Updates are free with your TAPE 16 license. License is lifetime.

Do I need an internet connection to use TAPE 16?

Recording and working on sessions is designed to happen locally on your machine. You need internet for license activation and checking/downloading updates.
