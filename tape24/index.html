What tools are built in?

TAPE 24 includes EQ, compression, automation, and additional plug-ins for shaping your sound. More built-in plug-ins are coming in future updates.

Does TAPE 24 use destructive recording?

Yes. Recording over a section replaces the audio, and there is no undo. This commit-first approach avoids storing hidden takes behind each edit, helping keep session files relatively small and easy to share. Sessions using the stock plug-ins can be opened without collaborators needing matching third-party tools.
