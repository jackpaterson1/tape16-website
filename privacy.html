Privacy Policy

This policy applies to TAPE 16, TAPE 24, the EMR Music Group website, licensing, purchases, support and related services.

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Your recordings stay yours

TAPE 16 and TAPE 24 store recordings, sessions, audio files and project data on your device or in a location you choose. EMR Music Group does not automatically upload your music, stems, takes or session files to its servers.

Audio or project files are only sent to us when you choose to provide them, such as by attaching files to a support request or submitting content to a community feature.

Information we may collect

Depending on the services you use, we may collect:

  • Purchase and licensing information, including your email address, order details, serial number, machine identifier, activation status and activation dates.
  • Support and feedback information, including your name, email address, app version, platform, message and any files you choose to attach.
  • Website usage information, such as pages visited, browser and device information, approximate location derived from an IP address, and interactions with the site.
  • Referral or affiliate information when you follow a tracked link or participate in the affiliate program.

How we use information

We use this information to process purchases, deliver and manage licences, provide downloads and updates, respond to support requests, improve TAPE products, prevent abuse, understand website performance and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

Payments and service providers

Payments are processed by third-party payment providers such as Stripe and PayPal. EMR Music Group does not receive or store your full payment card details.

We may use service providers for website hosting, licensing, email delivery, analytics, downloads, payment processing and affiliate attribution. These providers may process information on our behalf under their own privacy terms.

Cookies and local storage

The website may use cookies or browser storage for essential site functions, account sessions, checkout, analytics and referral attribution. You can control cookies through your browser settings, although blocking them may affect some features.

Retention and security

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain purchase and licence records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and meet legal requirements.

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No online service or storage system can guarantee complete security.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct or delete personal information held by EMR Music Group. Some purchase, tax, fraud-prevention or licensing records may need to be retained where required for legitimate business or legal reasons.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the products and services change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email support@emrmusicgroup.com.
